# Kitchen Buddy custom meal photography standard

Decision recorded 2026-10-20.

- Every meal photo should be created specifically for Kitchen Buddy. Stop introducing internet/stock recipe photographs into the recipe library.
- Aim for natural, appetizing home-cooked food photography: warm indirect light, realistic ingredients, tasteful rustic plates, consistent crop/aspect ratio (4:3), and no people, superimposed words, labels, artificial faces, or surreal food.
- Every photo must match its own named recipe rather than depict a vaguely related meal. Review visually before replacing any image.
- Keep custom recipe edits, favorites, user-uploaded photos, and offline state intact. Never replace user-supplied photography automatically.
- Existing external photos are transitional only until their verified custom replacements have been added to assets and linked in recipes. Do not point image URLs to missing assets.
- No claim that generated images have been installed in the live app until the file paths are committed and the rendered recipe is verified.
- New original recipe added: `kb-shrimp-okra-kidney-bean-pasta` (shrimp, okra, kidney beans, whole-wheat penne, mixed vegetables, Classico tomato sauce).
- Generated meal photos currently packaged for 32 recipe IDs; the corresponding image files still need to be uploaded into the repo before they can be activated. Eight more food photos remain to be created, excluding the timer test.
