## 5. Garlic Chicken & Cheesy Brown Rice

### [📷 See the cooked dish and recipe photographs — Unbound Wellness](https://unboundwellness.com/one-pot-broccoli-cheddar-chicken-and-rice/)

**Recipe:** [One Pot Broccoli Cheddar Chicken and Rice](https://unboundwellness.com/one-pot-broccoli-cheddar-chicken-and-rice/)  
**Recipe creator:** Michelle of Unbound Wellness. **Photo credit on original page:** [@Sierra_Inn](https://www.instagram.com/sierra_inn/) (the page identifies the photographer's handle).
**Why this is promising:** The cooking page includes a photograph of a completed pot of rice, broccoli, chicken and melted cheese, plus detailed cooking-process photos. It calls for garlic, broccoli, chicken, rice and cheddar.  
**Important differences:** The exact published recipe uses *jasmine rice* rather than brown rice, and *goat cheddar* rather than ordinary cheddar. Brown rice cooks differently; do not copy its 15-minute rice directions without testing and adapting for brown rice.  
**Action:** Review the finished dish and decide whether you prefer the goat-cheese/jasmine variation or a Kitchen Buddy adaptation. Seek image-reuse permission before storing its photograph in our app.

These are **source candidates only** — not finished replacements. The assistant can select credible dishes and adapt factual ingredients and cooking methods into original beginner-friendly timed steps, with source links recorded, without requesting per-photo or per-recipe approval. Copyright still protects their expressive wording and images, even if their recipes are free to read. For any picture, verify a license permitting our intended use or obtain the photographer/publisher's permission. A hyperlink alone doesn't authorize storing their photo in a hosted app.
